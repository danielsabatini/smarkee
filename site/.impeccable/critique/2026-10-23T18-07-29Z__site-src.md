#### What's Working

- **Confident Identity**: The use of terms like "A Nuvem Brasileira" and "Reconciliação Declarativa Ativa" coupled with strong emerald coloring immediately positions the product as serious and professional.
- **Responsive Layout**: The hero mock gracefully stacks on smaller screens, keeping information accessible.

#### Priority Issues

- **[P1] Jargon Barrier**: The hero's mock UI relies on internal acronyms (IPM, SUM, AEM, ODM) and terms like "Reconciliação Contínua" without enough plain language grounding.
  - **Why it matters**: First-time visitors will struggle to understand what the product actually does if they have to decode acronyms first.
  - **Fix**: Replace acronyms with clear benefit statements, or move the deep technical mock below a more plain-language introduction.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable clarify`
- **[P2] Visual Noise in Hero Mock**: The "fake UI" contains many small badges, pulsating dots, and dense text blocks that compete for attention.
  - **Why it matters**: It creates a high cognitive load (Wall of Options), making it hard for users to know where to look first.
  - **Fix**: Reduce the number of visible elements. Highlight one primary status and group the rest.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable distill`
- **[P2] Ad-hoc Typography**: The detector found multiple instances of `text-[10px]` and `text-[11px]` scattered across blog and product pages.
  - **Why it matters**: Breaks visual consistency and can cause accessibility issues for users needing larger text.
  - **Fix**: Standardize on `text-xs` or define a specific small step in the design system.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable typeset`
- **[P3] Competing CTAs**: Three distinct calls to action ("Entrar no Console", "Ver Loop de Controle", "CLI sk") appear immediately under the hero text.
  - **Why it matters**: Dilutes the primary action and increases decision fatigue.
  - **Fix**: Emphasize the primary CTA and demote the others to less prominent links.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable layout`

#### Persona Red Flags

- **Casey (Distracted Mobile User)**: The three side-by-side buttons in the hero will likely wrap into a tall block of decisions on mobile, and the dense mock UI will be difficult to scan quickly on a small screen.
