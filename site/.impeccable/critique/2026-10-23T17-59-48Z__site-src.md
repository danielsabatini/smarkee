### Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment**: The site aims for a clean, accessible Brazilian cloud identity. However, the presence of generated-UI signatures like gradient text on headings and grid-line backgrounds makes it feel less like a rigorous engineering tool and more like a generic AI-generated landing page. The brand explicitly calls for flat-by-default, clean canvas, and strong typographic hierarchy using weights, not gradients.

**Deterministic scan**: The detector found 51 issues, predominantly `10px` and `11px` font sizes hardcoded off the type ramp (which specifies `0.75rem` / 12px as the smallest `label` / `mono` size). It also flagged undocumented rgba colors in `app.css`, a decorative `codex-grid-background`, and `gradient-text` in `Hero.svelte`.

**Visual overlays**: No reliable user-visible overlay is available (browser automation unsupported in this environment).

### Overall Impression

The foundation is solid, but the execution relies on cheap visual tricks (gradients, grids) rather than the rigorous typographic hierarchy demanded by the `DESIGN.md`. The typography scale is also broken with too many micro-sizes (10px, 11px) causing accessibility and consistency issues. The single biggest opportunity is stripping the decorative noise to let the layout and typography lead.

### What's Working

- **Strict Brand Signal**: Emerald is generally reserved for action and signals, matching the DESIGN.md rules.
- **Component Foundation**: SvelteKit + Tailwind v4 structure gives a modern, fast base for the interactive simulators.

### Priority Issues

- **[P1] AI-generated UI tells (Slop)**
  - **Why it matters**: Gradient text in `Hero.svelte` and a grid-line background in `app.css` cheapen the "technical authority" the brand aims for, directly violating the "clean, flat-by-default" and "weight is the hierarchy" rules.
  - **Fix**: Remove `bg-clip-text` gradients and use solid `text-heading` (slate-900). Remove the grid background.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable distill`

- **[P1] Micro-typography outside ramp**
  - **Why it matters**: Dozens of `10px` and `11px` hardcoded font sizes across `BlogPreview`, `CodeSnippetTab`, and `ControlLoopInteractive`. Violates DESIGN.md scale, creates accessibility issues (below standard 12px minimum), and fragments consistency.
  - **Fix**: Replace with standard `text-xs` (0.75rem/12px) for mono/labels. Adjust container layouts if necessary.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable typeset`

- **[P2] Undocumented Colors**
  - **Why it matters**: Hardcoded `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)` in `app.css` and inline styles break the strict Emerald palette definition and introduce drift.
  - **Fix**: Use standard Tailwind utility classes mapping to `slate-50` or the documented palette.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`
